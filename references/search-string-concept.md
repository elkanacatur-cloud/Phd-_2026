# Search String Concept and Construction

> Internal reference for Study 1 (ILR). Records how the search string was built
> from three conceptual blocks. Source: `20260422_IR_Protocol_Elkana.docx`.

## Logic

The search is structured around three conceptual blocks that map onto the research
question: a **construct** (temporality), a **phenomenon** (conflict / misalignment),
and a **context** (megaprojects). Terms within a block are joined with `OR`; the three
blocks are joined with `AND`. A record must therefore carry at least one term from each
block to be retrieved. This design balances precision (relevance) and recall
(coverage) while keeping a transparent conceptual logic (Booth & Papaioannou, 2016;
Bramer et al., 2018; Snyder, 2019).

Truncation (`*`) retrieves grammatical and conceptual variants. `r*ythm*` is
constructed to capture both standard and variant spellings (rhythm / rythm).

## Block 1 — Temporality (construct)

Captures literature on temporality in organisational and project settings. Keywords
drawn from foundational works on temporal organisation (Geiger et al., 2021;
Omidvar et al., 2025; Pérez-Nordtvedt et al., 2008; Stjerne et al., 2019).

Terms: tempo, temporality, temporal, pacing, rhythm, time reckoning, temporal
orientation, entrainment.

```
(temporal* OR pacing OR tempo* OR r*ythm* OR "time reckoning" OR "temporal orientation" OR entrain*)
```

## Block 2 — Conflict and Misalignment (phenomenon)

Captures gaps, tensions, or disruptions between heterogeneous temporal systems.
Keywords from studies on inter-organisational temporal dynamics (Blagoev et al., 2024;
Casillas & Huising, 2025; Dille et al., 2023; Dille & Söderlund, 2011; Reinecke &
Ansari, 2015).

Terms: temporal misalignment, temporal misfit, asynchrony, temporal disruption,
temporal conflict, temporal tension, temporal miscoupling.

```
(misfit* OR misalignment* OR asynchron* OR disruption* OR conflict* OR tension* OR miscoupling*)
```

Anchored to Block 1 with `AND`, this retrieves compound constructs such as *temporal
misfit* and *temporal tension*.

## Block 3 — Megaprojects (context)

Built on the validated megaproject keyword list of Denicol et al. (2020), extended with
inter-organisational and institutional terms (Brookes et al., 2017; Dille & Söderlund,
2011) to retrieve studies conceptualising megaprojects as inter-institutional systems.

**Pilot-search refinement:** `infrastructure*` was removed after a pilot search. Although
widely used in megaproject research, it retrieved a large volume of irrelevant results
(e.g., colonisation and workforce literature) rather than megaproject development. This
evidence-based refinement is consistent with the iterative nature of IR methodology
(Snyder, 2019; Torraco, 2016).

## Combined structure

```
Block 1 (Temporality) AND Block 2 (Misalignment) AND Block 3 (Megaprojects)
```

Consistent with an integrative review approach, emphasis is placed on conceptual
coverage and theoretical sensitivity rather than exhaustive reporting of
database-specific Boolean syntax.

## Databases

Scopus and Web of Science, selected in combination for coverage across the disciplines
this topic requires.
