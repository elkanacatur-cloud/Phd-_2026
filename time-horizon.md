## Time Horizon

This research adopts a cross-sectional time horizon, meaning that it examines its phenomena as they stand across a bounded period of primary data collection rather than tracking the same units repeatedly as they change (Saunders et al., 2023). The primary data will be gathered within a window of roughly four months, so the design incorporates no repeated measurement of the same informants, institutions, or documents at successive intervals. The finite duration of a doctoral programme is one practical consideration behind this decision, but it is not the governing one, and the design does not rest on convenience. The decisive reason lies in the research question, which calls for an explanation of the generative mechanisms that link electoral cycles to project governance rather than a measured trajectory of change, and such mechanisms can be reconstructed analytically without observing a case in real time throughout its full course.

The object of this research is itself temporal, since it concerns the misalignment between electoral cycles and project timelines, and a cross-sectional design therefore invites a fair question about whether it can capture a process that unfolds across more than one electoral cycle. The design answers this by securing temporal depth analytically rather than through repeated observation: it reconstructs the sequence of governance decisions retrospectively, drawing on documentary sources that span successive electoral cycles and on the accounts of informants who held their positions during different administrations. What this reconstruction recovers is the ordering of events and the connections between them, that is, how one decision conditioned another, and not the repeated measurement of variables whose values are compared over time. This keeps the design consistent with the critical realist aim of explaining outcomes through the mechanisms that generated them, while remaining clear about what a single window of data collection can and cannot observe. The three studies that follow carry different temporal characters, and the next section explains how each contributes to this account: an integrative review, a set of cross-institutional interviews, and an in-depth case study.
